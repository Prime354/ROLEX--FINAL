Horological Engineering

The Art of Swiss Watchmaking

Every component of a Rolex watch is conceived, manufactured, and rigorously tested in Switzerland to exceed the world's most demanding standards of precision.

Swiss Movement

Manufacture Calibre 3235 self-winding mechanical movement with Chronergy escapement and blue Parachrom hairspring, offering exceptional resistance to magnetic fields and shocks.

Sapphire Crystal

Synthesized corundum virtually impervious to scratches, featuring the Cyclops magnifying lens over the date aperture with double anti-reflective coating for crystal clarity.

Waterproof Design

Pioneered by Rolex with the original Oyster case in 1926. Monobloc middle case carved from solid Oystersteel, hermetically sealed with the Twinlock winding crown to 100 meters.

Lifetime Reliability

Every timepiece carries the Superlative Chronometer certification (-2/+2 seconds per day accuracy after casing) backed by an international 5-year guarantee of horological excellence.
